## **🚀 Hackathon & User Community Features on the Landing Page**  

To make the **Hackathon & User Community** section **engaging, interactive, and dynamic**, we need to ensure that users feel excited to participate while also having an easy way to engage with other entrepreneurs and investors.  

### **🎯 Key Sections for Hackathon & Community on the Landing Page**  

### ✅ **1. Hackathon Banner (Hero Section)**  
🔹 **Big, Bold Header**: "🚀 Join the Next Startup Hackathon – Build, Innovate, Win!"  
🔹 **3D Animation**:  
   - **Startup Rocket Animation** (Launching effect when scrolling)  
   - **Glowing Countdown Timer** (For upcoming hackathons)  
   - **Confetti Effect** (For past hackathon winners)  
🔹 **Call-to-Action Buttons**:  
   - 📝 Register Now  
   - 📜 View Hackathon Rules  
   - 🎖 Past Winners  

---

### ✅ **2. Upcoming & Ongoing Hackathons Section**  
🔹 **Live Countdown Timers** for each event  
🔹 **Categories**: AI, FinTech, HealthTech, Web3, etc.  
🔹 **Rewards Display** (Prize pool, investment offers)  
🔹 **Featured Judges & Mentors**  
🔹 **3D Animation:**  
   - **Flip Card Effect on Hover** (Reveals event details)  
   - **Live Participants Counter** (Real-time updates)  

---

### ✅ **3. How It Works (Hackathon Process Guide)**  
🔹 **Step 1**: Sign Up & Choose a Track  
🔹 **Step 2**: Build Your Idea (Get Mentorship)  
🔹 **Step 3**: Pitch to Investors & Win Prizes  
🔹 **3D Animation:**  
   - **Interactive Steps Unfold on Scroll**  
   - **AI Chatbot Icon Pulsating (For hackathon help)**  

---

### ✅ **4. Leaderboard & Past Winners**  
🔹 **Top Teams** & Their Innovations  
🔹 **Investor-Funded Startups from Hackathons**  
🔹 **Leaderboard Animations:**  
   - **Names Scroll in a 3D Rotating Cylinder**  
   - **Trophy Icon Sparks for 1st Place**  

---

### ✅ **5. User Community (Forum & Networking Space)**  
🔹 **Entrepreneurs Can:**  
   - **Post Startup Ideas** (For feedback & co-founders)  
   - **Ask Business & Tech Questions**  
   - **Find Hackathon Teammates**  
🔹 **Investors Can:**  
   - **Discuss Startup Trends**  
   - **Upvote the Best Business Ideas**  
   - **Host AMA (Ask Me Anything) Sessions**  
🔹 **3D Animation:**  
   - **Live Chat Bubbles Float & Fade**  
   - **Upvotes Glow When Clicked**

### ✅ **6. Live Events & Webinars Section**

🔹 **Register for Pitching & Networking Events**

## **💬 Live Chat System – Features & Functionality**  

A **real-time live chat system** is essential for smooth communication between **startups, investors, and hackathon participants**. Below is a complete breakdown of how the **Live Chat** will work, including its **features, UI design, and animations**.

---

## **🎯 Live Chat System Features**  

### ✅ **1. Chat Categories**  
Users can chat in different categories:  
1️⃣ **One-on-One Chat** – Private chat between startup founders & investors  
2️⃣ **Group Chat** – Investors, mentors, and startups in topic-based rooms  
3️⃣ **Hackathon Chat** – Dedicated chat for teams during hackathons  
4️⃣ **Community Chat (Public Channels)** – Open discussions on startup trends  

📌 **🔒 Security**:  
- End-to-end encryption for **private chats**  
- **Spam detection & moderation** in public channels  

---

### ✅ **2. Chat UI & Design**  
📌 **Chat Window Layout:**  
- **Left Panel:** Contacts & Chat Categories  
- **Middle Panel:** Chat messages & media  
- **Right Panel:** User profile, files, pinned messages  

📌 **Key UI Elements:**  
- **Read Receipts** (✔️ Single = Sent, ✅ Double = Read)  
- **Typing Indicator** (User is typing...)  
- **Status Badges** (🟢 Online, 🟡 Away, 🔴 Busy)  
- **User Tagging (@mention in group chats)**  

📌 **🎨 Animations:**  
- **Messages Slide In** smoothly when sent  
- **Typing Indicator Pulses** when someone is typing  
- **New Messages Fade In** for better UX  

---

### ✅ **3. Advanced Messaging Features**  
💬 **Text, Images, Videos, & Files** – Share startup documents & pitch decks  
📌 **Pin Important Messages** – Keep key details on top  
📁 **File Sharing with Cloud Storage** – Secure storage for shared documents  
🔊 **Voice Messages** – Quick communication without typing  
🎥 **Live Video Chat Integration** – Investors & startups can pitch live  

📌 **🚀 Next-Level Additions:**  
- **Chat Polls** – Investors can vote on ideas  
- **Scheduled Messages** – Plan messages for different time zones  

---

### ✅ **4. AI-Powered Chatbot Assistant (Optional Feature)**  
🤖 **AI Startup Assistant:**  
- Suggests startup resources & investment tips  
- Helps founders improve pitch messages  
- Provides answers to common investor queries  

📌 **🎨 UI & Animation:**  
- Chatbot’s Avatar **blinks & moves** when responding  
- Smooth **typing animation** before AI replies  

---

### ✅ **5. Video & Voice Chat Feature (For Investor Pitches)**  
📌 **1-Click Video Calls** – Instant investor-founder discussions  
📌 **Screen Sharing** – Founders can present their pitch decks  
📌 **Record Meetings** – Save investor calls for future reference  

📌 **🎨 Animation & UI Effects:**  
- **Smooth Pop-Up Animation** for video calls  
- **Dynamic Mic & Camera Icons**  
- **Live Reaction Emojis** (👏, 👍, 🚀) during calls  

---

### ✅ **6. Notification System for Chat**  
🔔 **Instant Notifications:**  
- **New Message Alerts** (Popup + Sound)  
- **Missed Call Reminders**  
- **@Mention Alerts** (Highlight tagged messages)  

📌 **🎨 Animations:**  
- **Subtle Shake Effect** on new messages  
- **Bubble Pop Sound** when message arrives

### **🚀 Startup Founder (Entrepreneur) Idea Submission Process in StartupMatch**  

Entrepreneurs (startup founders) should have a **secure and structured** way to share their startup ideas with potential investors. Here’s how the idea submission and investor matchmaking process will work:  

---

## **📌 1️⃣ Idea Submission Flow (For Startup Founders)**  
### **Step 1: Enter Idea Details**  
📌 **Fields to Fill:**  
✅ Startup Name  
✅ Industry (Tech, AI, Healthcare, etc.)  
✅ Idea Overview (Brief summary of the startup)  
✅ Target Market & Problem Solved  
✅ Unique Selling Point (USP)  
✅ Revenue Model (How will it make money?)  
✅ Required Investment Amount (Funding request)  
✅ Upload Pitch Deck (PDF, PPT)  
✅ NDA Toggle (Public vs. Private Idea)  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **Live Text Preview:** As the user types, a formatted version of their pitch appears dynamically.  
- **Progress Bar Animation:** A smooth progress bar to track form completion.  

---

### **Step 2: NDA Protection (Optional)**  
📌 **Security Features:**  
✅ **Public Submission** – Basic idea is visible to all investors.  
✅ **NDA-Protected** – Investors must **sign an NDA** to view full details.  
✅ **Blockchain Timestamping** – Idea submission is recorded on a blockchain for proof of ownership.  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **Lock & Unlock Animation** – Clicking NDA toggle will show a lock closing/opening.  
- **Blurred Idea Preview for NDA-Protected Ideas**  

---

### **Step 3: AI SWOT Analysis (Auto-Generated Insights)**  
📌 **Features:**  
✅ AI will **analyze the startup idea** and provide:  
   - **Strengths** (What makes it unique?)  
   - **Weaknesses** (Potential risks)  
   - **Opportunities** (Market potential)  
   - **Threats** (Competitors, challenges)  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **AI Chat-Like Interface:** "Your startup has a **high potential in AI & Tech investment trends!** 🚀"  
- **Live SWOT Chart Appears Gradually**  

---

### **Step 4: Startup Idea Approval & Visibility**  
📌 **Visibility Settings:**  
✅ **Private (Admin Review Required)**  
✅ **Public (Visible to Investors)**  
✅ **Featured Startups (Paid or High-Potential Startups)**  

📌 **What Happens Next?**  
🚀 Startup will appear in the **Explore Startups** section for investors.  
📩 Founder gets notified when an investor **shows interest or signs NDA**.  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **Startup Card Appears in a 3D Grid with Other Startups**  
- **Floating Investor Icons Showing Real-Time Interest**  

---

## **📌 2️⃣ How Investors Can Show Interest?**  
✅ Investors can browse startup ideas & filter based on industry, funding needs, etc.  
✅ If NDA is required, they **must sign digitally** before accessing full details.  
✅ Investors can:  
   - **Upvote** (If they like the idea)  
   - **Message the Founder** directly  
   - **Send a Funding Proposal** (Investment offer with milestone breakdown)  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **Upvote Button Expands & Glows on Click**  
- **Chat Bubble Animation for Messages**  
- **Live Funding Bar Filling Up as Investors Commit Money**  

---

## **📌 3️⃣ Founder Dashboard (After Idea Submission)**  
✅ **Track Investor Interest** (Who viewed the idea, signed NDA, or messaged)  
✅ **Manage Investment Proposals** (Accept or negotiate offers)  
✅ **Update Startup Progress** (New milestones, funding rounds)  
✅ **Request AI-Powered Business Plan Review**  

📌 **3D Animation & UI Effect:**  
- **Investor Icons Sliding into “Interested” Section**  
- **Real-Time Updates for New Investment Proposals**

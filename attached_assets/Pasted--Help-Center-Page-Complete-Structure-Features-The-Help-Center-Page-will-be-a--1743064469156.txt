## **📌 Help Center Page – Complete Structure & Features**  

The **Help Center Page** will be a **self-service knowledge base** where users (Startup Founders, Investors, and Admins) can quickly find answers, guides, and support.  

✅ **User-friendly & intuitive design**  
✅ **Live chatbot & ticket support**  
✅ **Step-by-step guides & FAQs**  
✅ **Search functionality for quick solutions**  

---

## **🎨 Help Center Page Sections & Features**  

### **1️⃣ Hero Section (Quick Assistance)**  
📌 **Title:** "How Can We Help You Today?"  
📌 **Subtitle:** Get answers, explore guides, or connect with support  
📌 **Search Bar:** **Smart AI-powered search** for instant solutions  
📌 **CTA Buttons:**  
- 📖 **Browse FAQs**  
- 🎥 **Watch Tutorials**  
- 💬 **Live Chat Support**  

📌 **Visuals & Animations:**  
✅ Subtle fade-in animation for the search bar  
✅ Lottie animation of a support assistant  

---

### **2️⃣ Top Categories (Quick Navigation)**  
📌 **User-specific categories:**  
💡 **For Startups:** Submitting ideas, NDA process, funding security  
💰 **For Investors:** Exploring startups, escrow funding, ROI tracking  
🔧 **For Admins:** KYC verification, dispute handling, platform settings  

📌 **Visuals & Animations:**  
✅ **Category cards with hover effects** (icons change on hover)  
✅ **Smooth sliding animation when selecting a category**

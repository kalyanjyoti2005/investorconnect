### **⚙️ Advanced Settings Panel for StartupMatch Web App**  

The **Settings** page will be divided into multiple sections based on **user roles** (Startup Founder, Investor, and Admin). Each role will have access to specific **profile, security, notifications, and preferences** settings.  

---

## **🔹 General Settings (All Users)**  
📌 **Profile Settings**  
✔️ Change profile picture, name, and bio  
✔️ Update industry interests (Tech, Finance, AI, etc.)  
✔️ Change account role (if applicable)  

📌 **Account Visibility & Privacy**  
✔️ Choose profile visibility: **Public, Private, or NDA-Protected**  
✔️ Control who can message you (Everyone, Verified Users, Investors Only)  
✔️ Enable/Disable profile from appearing in startup/investor search  

📌 **Language & Theme**  
✔️ Dark/Light mode toggle  
✔️ Select app language (English, Hindi, etc.)  

---

## **🔹 Security Settings (All Users)**  
📌 **Password & Authentication**  
✔️ Change password (with OTP verification)  
✔️ View login history & active sessions  
✔️ Enable/Disable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**  

📌 **Device & Session Management**  
✔️ List of logged-in devices (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet)  
✔️ Option to **log out from all devices** remotely  
✔️ IP and location tracking for suspicious logins  

📌 **KYC & Verification**  
✔️ Upload ID proof (For investors & founders)  
✔️ Blockchain timestamp for **identity verification**  
✔️ Status of KYC: **Pending, Approved, Rejected**  

---

## **🔹 Investor-Specific Settings**  
📌 **Investment Preferences**  
✔️ Set investment range (e.g., **$10K – $500K**)  
✔️ Choose preferred industries (**Fintech, AI, Healthcare, etc.**)  
✔️ Enable automatic notifications for **new high-potential startups**  

📌 **Escrow & Payment Settings**  
✔️ Linked payment methods (Bank, UPI, Crypto, Stripe, PayPal)  
✔️ Auto-withdrawal settings for escrow fund releases  
✔️ Review transaction history & dispute resolution settings  

📌 **Investor Visibility Controls**  
✔️ Choose if startups can see your investment portfolio  
✔️ Control who can **pitch ideas** to you (All startups, Only NDA startups)  

---

## **🔹 Startup Founder-Specific Settings**  
📌 **Startup Profile & NDA Controls**  
✔️ Manage uploaded startup ideas  
✔️ Control NDA requirement (Mandatory/Optional)  
✔️ Restrict access to confidential documents  

📌 **Funding & Escrow Controls**  
✔️ Manage funding milestones & withdrawal requests  
✔️ Dispute resolution settings (Investor disputes, Admin intervention)  

📌 **Business Plan & AI Reports**  
✔️ Upload & edit **pitch deck, business plan, SWOT analysis**  
✔️ Enable AI-powered **funding success prediction**  

---

## **🔹 Notification & Messaging Settings**  
📌 **Email & Push Notifications**  
✔️ Enable/disable updates for **new messages, investment offers, funding progress**  
✔️ Set preferred contact method: **Email, SMS, App Notifications**  

📌 **Chat & Messaging Privacy**  
✔️ Allow only **verified investors/founders** to send messages  
✔️ Auto-delete old messages after **X days**  

📌 **Community & Hackathon Alerts**  
✔️ Subscribe to hackathon events & updates  
✔️ Get alerts when an investor or founder joins a discussion  

---

## **🔹 Admin-Only Settings**  
📌 **User & Role Management**  
✔️ Approve/reject **user KYC**  
✔️ Suspend or **blacklist fraudulent users**  

📌 **Transaction & Escrow Settings**  
✔️ Set **escrow holding period** before fund release  
✔️ Manage **payment disputes** and refund policies  

📌 **Platform Configuration**  
✔️ Adjust **transaction fees & revenue share**  
✔️ Manage **API keys & third-party integrations**  
✔️ Monitor **security logs & fraud detection**
